Materials we work with

Natural and engineered stone for kitchens, baths and any surface
that has to look good and last. Not sure which one fits? We will help.

  • Quartz

    Engineered and non-porous.
    No sealing, easy to clean.

  • Granite

    Natural stone that takes
    heat and daily wear well.

  • Marble

    Soft veining and a classic
    polished or honed finish.

  • Quartzite

    The look of marble with
    the hardness of granite.

  • Porcelain

    Thin, light slabs for modern
    kitchens and wall cladding.

How it works

Five steps from picking a slab to a finished kitchen.
You always know what happens next and when.

  1. 01

    Choose your stone

    Visit us or send a photo of the
    look you want. We help pick a
    slab or a remnant.

  2. 02

    Get a quote

    Send measurements or a drawing.
    We confirm price, edges and
    sink details in writing.

  3. 03

    Templating

    We measure on site and plan
    every seam and cutout before
    the slab is cut.

  4. 04

    Fabrication

    The slab is cut, edged and
    polished in our shop to the
    approved template.

  5. 05

    Installation

    Our crew installs the tops,
    connects the sink cutouts and
    cleans up after the job.
